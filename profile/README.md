| # | Pillar | Core Question | Focus |
|---|--------|---------------|-------|

## 🎯 P1. Customer & Problem Engineering
**Discover the right problem to solve.**

- 🗣️ Customer discovery and stakeholder interviews
- 🧩 Business process mapping and problem decomposition
- ❓ Requirements elicitation and ambiguity resolution
- ⚖️ Use-case prioritization, feasibility and ROI
- 📏 Success metrics and acceptance criteria

## 🔁 How the Pillars Fit Together

```
 P1 Discover ──▶ P2 Build ──▶ P3 Add Intelligence ──▶ P4 Enable Action
      ▲                                                      │
      │                                                      ▼
 P6 Deliver & Adopt ◀────────────── P5 Harden & Operate ◀────┘
```

1. **Discover** the right problem (P1)
2. **Build** a solid engineering foundation (P2)
3. **Apply GenAI** where it genuinely adds value (P3)
4. **Extend to agents** that act, within safe bounds (P4)
5. **Harden** for production: secure, observable, cost-aware (P5)
6. **Deliver** and drive adoption so value is realized (P6)

---

## 💡 Guiding Principles

- 🎯 **Problem first, technology second** — start from outcomes, not models.
- 🧱 **Reliability over novelty** — fine-tuning, multi-agent setups, and autonomy are used only *when justified*.
- 🛡️ **Bounded autonomy** — agents act within clear, tested limits.
- 📊 **Measure everything** — from acceptance criteria to production monitoring to business value.
- 🤝 **Adoption is the finish line** — working software isn't success until the customer wins.
